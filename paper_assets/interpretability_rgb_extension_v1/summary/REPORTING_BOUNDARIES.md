# Additional exploratory RGB interpretability analysis

Three RGB models were added after the original two-model analysis. All five training seeds and all 5,955 images were retained. The original 20 prediction-blind cases and every control placement were reused. No training or favourable selection occurred.

All five-model summaries use per-seed image means, sample SD, metric-specific denominators and 5,000 crossed seed x similarity-group bootstrap draws. Intervals are descriptive pointwise intervals, not confirmatory tests, not multiplicity-adjusted and not patient-level validation. Full LPT-minus-RGB contrasts compare complete systems; they do not isolate a causal prior effect. Archived predicted-class targets may differ across models; common true-label-target results are reported separately.

CAM target stages differ across architectures. Per-image normalized CAM appearance/overlap is not a common absolute attribution scale, anatomical accuracy, external clinical validity, or an explanation of the RA-LPT stack. FP32 changes and classification errors are retained. Zero/invalid CAMs are unavailable.
